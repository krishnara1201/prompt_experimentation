AG News — attribution and usage
================================

The AG News topic-classification dataset is derived from "AG's corpus of news
articles on the web" collected by the ComeToMyHead academic news aggregator
(http://www.di.unipi.it/~gulli/AG_corpus_of_news_articles.html).

It was constructed and popularized as a benchmark by:

    Xiang Zhang, Junbo Zhao, Yann LeCun (2015).
    "Character-level Convolutional Networks for Text Classification."
    Advances in Neural Information Processing Systems 28 (NeurIPS 2015).

The underlying AG corpus is provided for non-commercial research and
informational purposes only, per the ComeToMyHead terms.

Only a small stratified sample is vendored in this repository: 120 rows
(30 per class: World, Sports, Business, Sci/Tech) drawn from the Hugging Face
`fancyzhx/ag_news` test split by `fetch_ag_news.py`. The full corpus is not
redistributed here. Re-run `fetch_ag_news.py` to regenerate the sample.
